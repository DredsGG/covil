### What's new
- The app no longer opens to a black screen: if the server is slow you see "Connecting…" and, if it doesn't answer, "No connection" with "Try again".
- The Covil window always opens inside the screen, even after changing monitors or resolution.
- Long microphone and headphone names no longer break the Settings layout.
- Call audio stays on the chosen output when you switch calls, and all Covil sounds go there (just one "Covil" in the Windows Volume Mixer).
- The stream fps counter no longer drops to 0 for no reason; a still screen shows as "still image".
- "Playing now" no longer mistakes the Epic overlay (Epic Online Services) for a game.
- In the call list the name comes before the tag, and the channel column can be widened: drag its right edge.
- The desktop shortcut no longer loses its icon after an update.
- Flipping the camera skips a camera that won't open (in use by another program) and, if none opens, keeps the previous one.
