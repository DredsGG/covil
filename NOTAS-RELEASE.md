### What's new
- With several screens live, the left bar no longer pushes your panel and the call buttons off screen: the screen list scrolls inside.
- A new message in a conversation shows up on the left bar with the person's picture and the message count in the corner, like on Discord.
- The new version notice only shows up when the installer is ready to download.
- New Support ♥ button in the footer, optional.
